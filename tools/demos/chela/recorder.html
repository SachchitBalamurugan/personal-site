Chela take recorder

Records a webcam take of you blinking a word in Morse. Afterwards, the clip runs through Chela's real blink detector and Morse decoder, and the demo video shows what they decoded.

  1. Sit facing the camera in even light, with your whole face in frame. Glasses are fine if they don't glare.
  2. Turn your sound on. With your eyes shut you can't see the screen, so a tone plays for as long as you should keep your eyes closed. Close when it starts and open when it stops.
  3. It begins with two short clicks. Just blink normally at each one. Chela should ignore those blinks.
  4. When it's done, download the clip and send it over, or commit it to a branch (not main) as tools/demos/chela/take.webm.
